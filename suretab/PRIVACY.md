# SureTab Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

SureTab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **What is stored:** the addresses (URLs) and titles of the tabs you choose to save, your groups and your settings.
- **Where:** only on your device, in the browser's extension storage, and — if the "Automatic backup in bookmarks" option is on (default) — in a bookmarks folder named "SureTab backup" in your own browser. If you use Chrome Sync, Google syncs that folder like any other bookmark, under Google's privacy policy.
- **No servers:** SureTab has no backend, no account, no analytics and no advertising. It makes no network requests of its own. Favicons are shown through the browser's built-in favicon service.
- **Your control:** you can export or delete your data at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes its storage; the bookmark backup folder stays until you delete it.

## Description

Too many tabs open? Save them all into one list with a single click — and stop worrying about losing them.

SureTab is a OneTab alternative built around one promise: your saved tabs never disappear.

🛟 THREE SAFETY NETS
• Automatic backup in bookmarks: every saved group is mirrored to a "SureTab backup" bookmarks folder. It survives browser updates and "cleaner" apps, and syncs with your Google account. If your list is ever empty, SureTab finds the backup and restores it in one click.
• Trash: deleted groups and links stay recoverable for 30 days. Every delete has Undo.
• Automatic snapshots: go back to how your list looked hours ago.

⚡ SAVE AND RESTORE
• Save all tabs in this window, in all windows, or just one tab — from the toolbar, the right-click menu or Alt+Shift+S
• Restore a whole group or a single link, in this window or a new one
• Restored tabs don't load until you open them, so they use almost no memory
• Search, rename and pin groups to the top

## Permission justifications (dashboard fields)
- **tabs**: read the URL and title of the tabs the user chooses to save, close them after saving, and open them again on restore.
- **storage / unlimitedStorage**: store the user's saved tab lists locally; users may save thousands of tabs.
- **bookmarks**: keep an automatic backup copy of the saved lists in a "SureTab backup" bookmarks folder, and restore from it if the list is lost.
- **contextMenus**: "Save this tab / Save all tabs" items in the right-click menu.
- **favicon**: show each saved site's icon in the list.
- Remote code: No. Data collection: none (tick nothing; certify the disclosures).
